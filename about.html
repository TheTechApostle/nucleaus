Our Story

Born from a Bold Belief: Africa Deserves Better Tech.

Nucleus Tech Solution was founded in Lagos, Nigeria, with a singular belief: that the world's digital transformation would only happen when Africans built it themselves — with world-class education, world-class products, and world-class enterprise services.

We are not just a company — we are a carefully engineered ecosystem. Three arms. One purpose. One nucleus.

The world's next-gen tech school — training 5,000+ professionals in the skills that matter most.

Nucleus Studios

A product innovation hub building scalable platforms for FinTech, HealthTech, AgriTech & EdTech.

Enterprise digital services — cloud, security, and custom software for businesses worldwide.
